# Explainer beat grammar

Every duration is a fixed number of **beats** — units of one idea. Scenes usually map 1:1 to beats; a long mechanism beat may span two scenes.

## Archetypes

### 6s — Sting (2 beats)
1. **Reveal** (~60–70%): mark/logo/one line enters with the single showpiece move (spring or overshoot). Optional background flash for energy.
2. **Hold & out** (~30–40%): everything rests ≥30f, then fade or veil out.

Use for: bumpers, end tags, section breaks.

### 15s — Social punch (3 beats)
1. **Hook** (~35%): one number or one claim, biggest thing on screen, hero entrance.
2. **Support** (~40%): the qualifier — what the number means. Underline/highlight motion, restaged layout.
3. **CTA** (~25%): logo + imperative line + pill. End card holds ≥45f.

Use for: paid social, reels/shorts, stat drops. 9:16 first — design in the vertical safe zone (captions live bottom 15%).

### 30s — Explainer (5 beats)
1. **Hook** (~15%): the tension as a question or bold claim. Text-led.
2. **Context/tension** (~20%): the world of the problem — the two systems, the mess, the manual step.
3. **Mechanism** (~30%, the longest): the thing actually working. LITERAL motion: data travels, pieces connect, states change. This beat earns the video.
4. **Payoff** (~15%): the after-state; calm, settled, one satisfying spring.
5. **CTA** (~20%): brand line + action. Hold ≥45f.

### 60s — Deep dive (6–8 beats)
As the 30s plus: a **worked example** beat after mechanism (concrete instance with real-ish data) and an **objection** beat before CTA ("what about X?" answered in one line). Nothing else grows — resist padding hook/context.

## Rhythm notation

Declare before animating: `HOOK(hold) → context(fast) → MECHANISM(slow, literal) → payoff(spring, settle) → CTA(hold)`. Uppercase = where the energy peaks. If two adjacent beats have the same energy, retime one.

## Platform pacing

| Platform | First read | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Paid social (9:16) | Hook must land by 1.5s | assume muted; text carries everything |
| LinkedIn feed (1:1/16:9) | by 2s | calmer easing reads as more credible |
| Website hero (16:9) | by 2.5s | may loop; make last→first frame compatible |
| Product page embed | n/a | viewer chose to watch; mechanism can breathe |

## Scene-count math

`beats ≈ scenes`. At 30fps: 30s = 900f minus crossfade overlaps. Distribute by the percentages above, round to whole frames, then give whatever slack remains to the mechanism beat. Scene minimum 45f (1.5s) — anything shorter reads as a glitch unless it's an intentional hard-cut accent.
